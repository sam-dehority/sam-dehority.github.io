### References and topics

The goal of the seminar is to understand specific examples of integrable systems with a view towards their relationship with supersymmetric gauge theories and 
enumerative geometry. More specifically:

1. Spin chains, lattice models, Bethe ansatz. There are many resources for this. A few good ones are
[https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.5031](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.5031), and
the introduction to Jimbo, Miwa *Algebraic Analysis of Solvable Lattice Models*.
2. Relationship with supersymmetric vacua/topological theories/enumerative geometry.
Key papers are
Nekrasov-Shatashvilli [https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4748](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4748), [https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4744](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4744)
from a physical perspective and the mathematical papers by
Aganagic-Okounkov [https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.08746](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.08746) and Pushkar, Smirnov, Zeitlin [https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08723](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08723)
3. At some point it would be good to see talks on the KdV, KP, and Toda lattice hierarchies. E.g. from Date, Jimbo, Miwa *Solitons: Differential Equations, Symmetries and Infinite Dimensional Algebras*.
4. Other approaches including the sequence Costello-Witten-Yamazaki [https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.09993](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.09993) [https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.01579](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.01579) and Costello-Yamazaki [https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02289](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02289).
