The goal of this seminar is to understand twistor theory and some of its relations to 
other parts of mathematical physics.


### Time and location information

Organisers: Sam DeHority, Zoe Himwich, Davis Lazowski  
Time/date: Wednesday 11am-12:15pm EST  
Location:  Math 622 

Please email  [zmh2110@columbia.edu](mailto:zmh2110@columbia.edu) if you would like to be on the 
mailing list.

###  Plan/references

Beginning with an introduciton to twistors and spinors, the plan is to understand some parts of 
1. Scattering amplitudes and their relation to holomorphic curves in twistor space, starting from the work 
[https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0312171](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0312171) and other developments including [https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0402045](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0402045), [https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.0885](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.0885). 
2. Developments in ambitwistor theory, perhaps from [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.2564.pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.2564.pdf) [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.05636.pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.05636.pdf). 
3. Understanding the quantization of certain twistor field theories in [https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.08879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.08879) and [https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02595) after a slow introduction to the BV formalism.
